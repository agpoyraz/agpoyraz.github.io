I am a Research and Development Manager with a PhD, specializing in machine vision, industrial metrology, image processing, and industrial inspection systems.

My research focuses on precision measurement, sub-pixel edge detection, robust geometric estimation, and machine vision applications for quality control in manufacturing.

I have experience in developing industrial machine vision systems, conducting academic research, and leading R&D projects in collaboration with industry and academia.

## Research Interests

- Machine Vision
- Industrial Metrology
- Image Processing
- Precision Measurement Systems
- Sub-pixel Edge Detection
- Geometric Fitting and Robust Estimation
- Quality Control and Inspection Systems

## Current Focus

My recent work includes diameter measurement accuracy improvement, circle fitting with outlier detection, concentricity estimation, and machine vision-based inspection solutions for industrial applications.
